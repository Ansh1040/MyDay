# Elsewhere — product brief (owner's requirements, condensed)

A polished personal app for exploring curiosity, breaking routines, developing skills, creating things, and understanding how I change over time. A personal playground with meaningful experiences and practical tools — **not** a productivity dashboard and **not** an endless feed of images/videos.

**Design: "Index"** — clean, modern, editorial mobile app; strong typography, spacious layouts, curated discovery cards, clear navigation, subtle accents. Four themes (Vermilion: warm ivory + tomato red; Cobalt: cool white + blue with prominent search & topic filters; Plum: warm neutrals + berry, thoughtful editorial; Graphite: minimal monochrome + small burnt-orange highlights), each with day, night and automatic appearance. Switching themes keeps all data and functionality.

**Core loop:** Discover → explore or try → create or act → reflect → connect discoveries → better suggestions. Any area can be entered directly; no fixed sequence. Example: explore why music affects mood → try a listening experiment → learn to make a soundscape → take a sound-hunting walk → record what changed in Version History.

## Four core areas
- **Rabbit Hole** — questions, topics, branching paths; connected ideas and unexpected discoveries; interactive experiments, quizzes, simulations, explanations; investigate different perspectives; related experiences and practical follow-ups; save discoveries and resume unfinished explorations.
- **Plot Twist** — break routines with personalised surprises: small real-world adventures, creative prompts, unusual activities; based on interests, available time, energy, budget, comfort; adjustable challenge intensity; solo and shared; capture the experience and reflect afterwards.
- **Skill Arcade** — skill selection & learning paths; beginner entry points; short practice; hands-on projects; levels and visible progress; deeper challenges; saved work & easy continuation; connects learning, curiosity and real-world application. Progress = meaningful participation and learning.
- **Version History** — personal timeline of reflections, opinions, interests, goals, milestones; notes about changing my mind; earlier vs current perspectives; revisit past entries; connect to completed experiences/projects; personal capsules and future messages; patterns to explore without presenting speculation as fact.

## Additional features
- **Curiosity Map** — interactive map connecting explored topics, creations, skills, reflections, experiences; navigate relationships.
- **Remix Studio** — turn one discovery into a new experience (article → experiment, reflection → story, skill → real-world challenge, exploration → creative project).
- **Personal Capsules** — save predictions, opinions, voice notes, messages or creations to reopen later through Version History.
- **Surprise Dial** — choose how far outside the comfort zone suggestions go, familiar → adventurous.
- **Experience Builder** — create quests, quizzes, experiments, challenges, learning journeys; private or for friends.
- **Field Notes** — quickly capture text, voice, sketches, photos, links; connect to topics, projects, quests, reflections.
- **Two-Person Quests** — shared challenges, comparing interpretations, collaborative small projects.
- **Return Tickets** — save unfinished explorations with where I stopped, what caught my attention, what I wanted to try next; easy resume.
- **Perspective Switch** — explore a question through different viewpoints; record whether my thinking changed.
- **Personal Collections** — named collections, e.g. "Things that surprised me", "Projects I want to try", "Ideas I changed my mind about".

## Content is open-ended
Interactive experiments, audio/listening, articles, quizzes, simulations, games, writing prompts, sketching, coding exercises, hands-on projects, real-world challenges, outdoor exploration, conversations/debates, collaborative activities, reflection exercises, future formats. Choose the best format per experience; new formats must be addable without rebuilding the core.

## Personalisation & discovery
Light onboarding: interests, skills to develop, goals, available time, energy, comfort level, preferred formats, theme & appearance. Personalised For You feed; search; topic, time/energy and format filters; Surprise Me; saved; "More like this", "Less like this", "Hide this". Suggestions improve via explicit preferences and feedback. Explain whether recommendations are curated, rule-based or generated.

## Interaction
Mobile-first, responsive for larger screens, smooth touch, useful swipe gestures with visible alternatives, immersive views where appropriate, clear back navigation, comfortable touch targets, safe areas, accessible text/controls, keyboard support, reduced motion.

## Timers & notifications
Timers for timed experiments, skill practice, creative sessions, real-world challenges: start, pause, resume, reset, completion; accurate across navigation/backgrounding. Notification controls for timer completion, chosen reminders, capsule availability, shared activity updates, returning to saved journeys. Contextual permission requests; adjustable reminders; quiet controls; clear explanation of platform limits; distinguish foreground notifications from ones that work while the app is closed.

## Supporting features
Profile & preferences; search, filtering, bookmarking, collections; progress tracking; drafts & autosave; loading/empty/success/error states; reliable persistence; export of personal data; editing and deletion controls; privacy controls for personal and shared content; offline access; clear handling of unavailable network features; installation guidance. Never present simulated behaviour as working functionality.

## Visual do/don't
Do: refined editorial feed, strong readable type, generous spacing, clear hierarchy, subtle dividers, curated cards with varied layouts, native-feeling controls, consistent icons, restrained animation, readable day/night palettes.
Avoid: corporate dashboard styling, decorative analytics or invented statistics, clutter, desktop UIs squeezed into phones, excessive glow/gradients, large decorative artwork, restricting discovery to a media gallery.
